# Genesis holder presentation and purchase receipt

The holder presentation adds a gold holder badge and page-wide mineral textures. Dark mode retains the black/lime theme; light mode retains cream/blue. Existing UVEL artwork, empty state, holdings, pre-listing allocation, post-listing emissions and six holder rights remain driven by their existing stores.

The holder card directly displays the raster backgrounds `src/static/img/genesis/vip-card-dark.png` and `vip-card-light.png` with centered `background-size: cover` and an 18px corner radius. The dark artwork combines obsidian, gold and the brand lime `#9EDC1D`; the light artwork combines ivory, obsidian, gold and the brand blue `#0E48E6`. The real UVEL logo and live card content remain separate from the artwork.

The transparent `vip-card-aura.png` supplies the visible champagne-gold bloom and soft shadow. A single non-interactive pseudo-element uses nine-slice `border-image` without a center fill; the original card artwork and live content remain clear. Fixed corner scaling preserves the light shape as post-listing content increases the card height. CSS-generated shadows, old SVG corners and masks are absent. The lighting PNG is copied byte-for-byte from the built-in imagegen output; its central opening and outer corners contain real alpha transparency.

The six seat benefits form a static two-column description section. Each benefit displays a plain gold icon, its existing localized title and its complete description. Individual benefits have no button styling, focus target or activation handler; all descriptions are readable without opening a dialog. Separate purchase, marketplace and staking actions remain interactive. An emissions explanation link at the page bottom opens `/pages/genesis/how-it-works` both before and after listing, independently of the removed listing-progress display.

Asset provenance: the backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The tool does not expose the exact model identifier.

The pending purchase sheet cannot be dismissed and reopened to reset its submission guard. The success dialog reuses the existing focus-management helper.

No prices, eligibility, settlement rules, allocation or emission calculations changed. The current frontend uses fixed mock mode; browser purchases below run only in disposable accounts. This is not evidence of a production payment transaction. No economic PRD changes are required for this presentation and receipt-navigation change.

## Image generation prompts

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool; the interface does not expose a model selector or confirm `chatgpt-image-2.5`. The dark and light card backgrounds were generated without reference images. Those two backgrounds were then supplied as style references for the separate transparent lighting artwork. All PNG outputs are copied without edits.

### Transparent lighting surround

Use case: ui-mockup / lighting asset. Input image 1 and image 2 are STYLE REFERENCES for the app's approved dark obsidian/gold/lime and light pearl/gold/blue VIP cards. Generate ONLY a reusable transparent PNG LIGHTING SURROUND for those existing cards, not a new card face. The output is a front-facing flat rectangular aura and soft contact shadow with REAL alpha transparency, a fully TRANSPARENT large central opening and transparent outer background. No checkerboard painted in the pixels. Canvas landscape 1536x1024. The virtual rounded card silhouette is inset about 64 pixels from each canvas edge, corners radius about 72 pixels; the entire large interior must remain empty/transparent. Gold light is concentrated immediately around this virtual rounded rectangle with a luxurious soft champagne-gold bloom fading gradually outward into transparent pixels, together with a soft charcoal contact shadow cast below it. Make the glow plainly noticeable when shrunk to a 350px-wide phone card, around THREE TIMES more visible than an almost imperceptible CSS shadow, yet polished and tasteful. A few smooth local brighter gold reflections across the curved corners create a premium physical warm-metal feel, not a uniformly bright neon stroke. Most illumination lives outside the silhouette, a gentle small inward reflection is fine. No sharp full outline, no angular streaks, no sparkles, no particles, no rays, no explosions, no ring of opaque yellow, no fake gray or black matte backdrop. Keep straight edge lighting visually smooth and consistent so the image can be used as a 9-slice scalable frame without seams. The center must be transparent, with NO card surface, text, logo, number, badge, icon, material pattern or white panel. This bitmap supplies the actual glow AND drop shadow over the application's existing dark or light page texture; all existing card artwork and app text will remain separate and visible. High-end softly feathered gold luminance with real alpha falloff, substantial but restrained dimensional lift.
